01 / Fact sheet

Six things, all sourced.

The animal
Tigrino, male, ~10 years, ~1.4 kg (3 lb), Leopardus tilcayo. Holotype. The only confirmed living individual of the species. Taken from the wild as a kitten and kept as a pet for ~1 year; at La Senda Verde Wildlife Sanctuary, Yungas, Bolivia, since 2017, non-releasable. Sanctuary asks that the story not be used to romanticize keeping wild cats as pets.
The roles
La Senda Verde rescued Tigrino and has cared for him for almost ten years. Biologist Paola Nogales-Ascarrunz (Programa de Investigación de Félidos Bolivia) and an international team provided the science identifying the species. Distinct and equally important; please credit both.
The species
Leopardus tilcayo, described 17 Sep 2026 in Current Biology (Lescroart, Nogales-Ascarrunz et al.). First new living cat species since the pampas cat, 1923. Diverged from other tiger cats ~1.4 million years ago.
The token
$TIGRINO, Solana, launched on pump.fun 18 Sep 2026 09:06 UTC. Creator fee split locked at 100% to La Senda Verde Animal Refuge Inc / 0% creator. Creator holds 0 tokens. Community-run.

$147,514 (1401 SOL) across 109 settled donations to La Senda Verde Animal Refuge Inc (US 501(c)(3), EIN 47-2415812), as of Mon, 19 Oct 2026 03:05:45 GMT. Live, unaudited; source donate.gg.
The sanctuary
La Senda Verde, founded 2003 by Vicky Ossio and Marcelo Levy, near Coroico, Bolivia. 1,000+ animals rescued from trafficking. sendaverde.org · info@sendaverde.org. Has agreed to speak to press (EN/ES).
Confirmation
Sanctuary confirmed receipt on X (18 Sep), posted a photo of Tigrino with a printed thank-you (19 Sep), and a 3-minute video of co-founder Marcelo Levy with Tigrino (19 Sep). Links below.
