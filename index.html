02

The species

Meet Leopardus Tilcayo

Tigrino arrived at La Senda Verde sanctuary in 2017, assumed to be a common tiger cat. Years later, thirty-eight genomes showed his line split from every other tiger cat about 1.4 million years ago. In September 2026 his species was formally described as Leopardus tilcayo.

Tigrino is his name. Tilcayo is the species. $TIGRINO is the community meme token that celebrates them both on Solana.
