02 / The story

Nine years in a sanctuary before anyone knew what he was.

  1. 2016

    A wild kitten is taken from the Yungas forest and kept as a pet for about a year, fed food that doesn't meet his species' needs. Whatever the intention, a wild animal was removed from his habitat.

  2. 2017

    He arrives at La Senda Verde. After a year in a household, release is no longer possible; the sanctuary takes responsibility for him for life. Staff assume a common tiger cat and name him Tigrino. His size raises questions, so they call biologist Paola Nogales-Ascarrunz.

  3. 2019

    Writing a field guide, she notices his spots match nothing from Brazil. “This is so wrong.”

  4. 2020–26

    Thirty-eight genomes across the genus. His line split from every other tiger cat 1.4 million years ago.

  5. 17 Sep 2026

    Leopardus tilcayo is published. First new cat species in 103 years. Tigrino is the holotype, the one animal the species is formally described from, and the only confirmed living tilcayo.

  6. 18 Sep 2026

    $TIGRINO launches with 100% of its creator fees locked to his sanctuary. By evening the sanctuary posts a thank-you.

  7. 19 Sep 2026

    $100,000 crossed. His keeper prints a sign. “Tigrino” trends on X by name.

06 / Questions

Short answers.

Who is Tigrino?+

A male wild cat, about 10 years old and 3 lbs. Taken from the forest as a kitten and kept as a pet for about a year, he arrived at La Senda Verde Wildlife Sanctuary in 2017 unable to return to the wild; the sanctuary has cared for him since. In September 2026 scientists confirmed he belongs to Leopardus tilcayo, a species never described before. He is the holotype and the only confirmed living Leopardus tilcayo; scientists believe others live undetected in the Yungas cloud forest.

What is Leopardus tilcayo?+

A small wild tiger cat species from Bolivia's Yungas cloud forest, described on September 17, 2026 in Current Biology and announced by National Geographic. The first new living cat species named since the pampas cat in 1923.

Tigrino vs tilcayo?+

Tigrino is the cat's name, given by sanctuary staff in 2017 when they believed he was a Leopardus tigrinus. Tilcayo is the species name, from the word Yungas locals have always used for the animal. One cat, two names.

Who rescued him and who discovered the species?+

Two distinct roles. La Senda Verde rescued Tigrino and has cared for him for almost ten years. Biologist Paola Nogales-Ascarrunz and an international research team provided the science that identified Leopardus tilcayo. The sanctuary asks that his story never be used to romanticize keeping wildlife as pets: he belongs to a wild species, and his place should have been in the forest.

How much has been donated?+

Over $100,000 (more than 1,000 SOL) within 48 hours, to La Senda Verde Animal Refuge Inc, the sanctuary's US 501(c)(3). In their words it goes to “Tigrino and more than 1,000 rescued animals.” The counter on this page reads directly from donate.gg. The sanctuary confirmed receipt on X, in a photo, and on video.

What is $TIGRINO?+

A community-run memecoin on Solana launched on pump.fun on September 18, 2026. 100% of its creator fees are locked to the sanctuary; the creator receives 0% and holds no tokens. No team, no roadmap, no affiliation with National Geographic or the study authors. The price can go to zero.

Is this the sanctuary's website?+

No. This is a community site. The sanctuary is sendaverde.org and @La_Senda_Verde on X. They know about this project and have publicly acknowledged it, but they don't run it.
